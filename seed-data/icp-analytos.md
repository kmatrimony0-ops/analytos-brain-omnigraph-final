# ICP for Analytos Products

This document summarizes target customers, personas, trigger signals, and opening angles for Analytos GTM motions.

## Stockly ICP Segments
- Inventory-heavy retailers: Retailers with seasonal demand, SKU sprawl, and frequent replenishment exceptions.
- Multi-branch distributors: Distribution teams with supplier lead-time changes and fragmented branch inventory views.

## Inspectly ICP Segments
- Regulated medical manufacturing teams: Organizations that need traceable inspection evidence and corrective action workflows.
- Distributed field quality teams: Teams performing inspections across sites with inconsistent reporting quality.

## Personas
- Inventory Operations Director: Cares about stock availability, planner productivity, and working capital.
- Demand Planning Manager: Cares about explainable forecasts, demand exceptions, and weekly planning cycles.
- VP of Quality: Cares about audit readiness, corrective action closure, and defect trend visibility.
- Quality Operations Manager: Cares about consistent inspection execution and evidence capture.

## Trigger Signals
- Frequent stockout escalations or emergency replenishment meetings.
- Manual forecast reviews across many spreadsheets.
- Upcoming compliance audit or recent corrective-action backlog.
- Rapid expansion into new stores, branches, or inspection sites.

## Competitor Displacement Angles
- Replace spreadsheet-heavy planning with governed, traceable recommendations.
- Replace disconnected inspection forms with one evidence-backed quality workflow.
