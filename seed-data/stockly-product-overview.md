# Stockly Product Overview

Product: Stockly

Stockly is an AI inventory intelligence product for mid-market retailers and distributors that need fewer stockouts, cleaner replenishment decisions, and better visibility across fast-moving SKUs.

## Features
- Demand Forecasting: Predicts SKU-level demand using sales velocity, seasonality, and recent operational signals.
- Reorder Automation: Suggests reorder quantities and timing so planners can act before stockouts happen.
- Stockout Risk Alerts: Flags locations and SKUs likely to run below safety stock in the next planning window.
- Supplier Lead-Time Monitor: Tracks supplier delays and highlights replenishment risks before purchase orders are late.

## Proof Points
- Pilot teams reduced stockout incidents by 18% after using Stockly alerts for replenishment planning.
- Inventory planners saved 6 hours per week by replacing spreadsheet checks with automated reorder recommendations.
- Forecast review cycles moved from 5 days to 2 days for priority categories.

## ICP Segments
- Mid-market omnichannel retailers: 50 to 500 store or warehouse locations with seasonal stockout risk.
- Regional distributors: Teams managing thousands of SKUs across branches with supplier lead-time variability.

## Personas
- Director of Inventory Operations: Owns stock availability, working capital, and replenishment performance.
- Demand Planning Manager: Needs explainable forecasts and fewer manual spreadsheet reviews.
