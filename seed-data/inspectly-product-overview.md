# Inspectly Product Overview

Product: Inspectly

Inspectly is an AI-assisted quality inspection and compliance product for regulated operations teams that need faster inspections, better audit trails, and fewer missed defects.

## Features
- Inspection Template Builder: Converts recurring quality procedures into reusable digital inspection templates.
- Computer Vision Triage: Highlights likely defects in uploaded inspection images for faster reviewer attention.
- Audit Trail Timeline: Preserves evidence, reviewer notes, approvals, and timestamps for compliance review.
- Corrective Action Tracker: Links inspection findings to owner, due date, status, and remediation evidence.

## Proof Points
- QA teams reduced inspection preparation time by 35% using reusable templates.
- Reviewers cut defect triage time by 22% when using image-based prioritization.
- Corrective-action follow-up completion improved to 91% within target SLA during pilot workflows.

## ICP Segments
- Medical device manufacturers: Regulated teams that need inspection evidence and corrective-action tracking.
- Field service quality teams: Distributed inspection teams needing standardized reports and faster review cycles.

## Personas
- VP of Quality: Accountable for audit readiness, compliance posture, and defect reduction.
- Quality Operations Manager: Needs consistent inspection execution and clear corrective-action ownership.
